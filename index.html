About

The name is the argument.

Every letter names a commitment. Together they describe a group that intends to move the field rather than follow it, and that lets the Global South claim a research frontier first.

AAdvancinga forward posture
HHuman capabilitiesreal freedoms, not only productive value
EEducationthe home and centre of gravity
AAnalyticsthe working method
DDevelopmentthe purpose

Vision

To be a world-leading, Global South-anchored, transdisciplinary research group advancing human capabilities through education, data and intelligent technologies, with integrity and equity at its core.

Mission

To generate rigorous evidence, tools and capacity that help universities, education systems, governments and employers use AI and data to expand learning, success and human development, so that intelligent technologies reduce rather than reproduce inequality.

Why "capabilities", not "capital". The group engages the language of human capital, which remains influential in policy and among funders, but engages it critically. Following Sen's own distinction, the skills that aid production are treated as a real but subordinate part of the wider goal of expanding what people are actually able to do and to be. Equity and transformation are constitutive of the work, not an afterthought.

AI at the centre. Generative AI, machine learning, predictive and learning analytics, and data science are what the group studies and what it works with. The capability approach supplies the values that steer that work toward equity and justice.

    Complementary by design

    Wits AHEAD is the only entity in the School that takes AI, analytics and data as both research object and method, the only one concentrating on higher education and human capability, and the only one that spans faculties. It complements the School's other research entities and absorbs no one.

    We are open to

    Fellows, candidates and partners.

    Wits AHEAD is actively accepting expressions of interest in three forms. Each is read by the Director and answered within ten working days.

    01

    Postdoctoral fellowships

    Postdoctoral fellows whose research uses artificial intelligence, analytics or data to advance human capability in higher education. Hosted in the Wits School of Education under the Wits postdoctoral policy, with co-supervision opportunities and a place in the group's publication programme.

    Express interest
    02

    PhD candidates

    Doctoral candidates whose questions sit on one of the six pillars: generative AI in higher education, analytics for student success, STEM and teacher education, graduate capability and just transitions, data for education systems, or responsible AI and policy. Apply through the Wits School of Education and name Wits AHEAD in your supervisory arrangements.

    See the pillars
    03

    Collaborations and partnerships

    Co-investigators from any Wits faculty, universities across Africa and beyond, government, industry and funders. Consortium bids, commissioned analytics, visiting fellowships and joint supervision.

    Propose a partnership
    04

    Research affiliations, North and South

    We are open to research affiliations and collaborations across the Global North and the Global South, for the sole purpose of building forward: shared studies, joint supervision, exchange of fellows and co-authored outputs that strengthen both partners and keep the Global South at the table where the field is being defined.

    Propose an affiliation
