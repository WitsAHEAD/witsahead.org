Postdoctoral and visiting fellowships

Join Wits AHEAD as a funded fellow.

For researchers within a few years of their doctorate whose work uses artificial intelligence, analytics or data to advance human capability in higher education. Bring your question to Johannesburg, and we will help you find the funding to do it.

What a fellowship here offers

A place in a working research group, not a desk on its own.

01

Hosted at the Wits School of Education

Fellows are hosted in the Wits School of Education under the Wits postdoctoral policy, at one of Africa's leading research universities.

02

Supervision and co-supervision

Regular supervision with the Group Director, and opportunities to co-supervise the group's doctoral and master's candidates.

03

A place in the publication programme

Fellows join the group's publication programme: shared studies, co-authored papers and proposals that carry on after the fellowship.

04

Scholars and partners from around the world

Time with visiting AHEAD Global Scholars and with the group's partners across Africa and beyond.

05

Help with the application

If the fit is right, we work on the application with you, including the host's part that most schemes ask for.

Funding

Schemes that can bring you to Wits.

Each of these schemes allows a fellow to be hosted at Wits and is open to education research. Which one fits depends on where you are now. Funding is not a condition for a first conversation.

From Europe

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships: Global Fellowships

European Commission · for European nationals or long-term residents, up to eight years after the doctorate

Spend 12 to 24 months with Wits AHEAD as your host outside Europe, then a 12-month return phase at a European university that applies with you.

2027 call: planned to open 7 April 2027, deadline 8 September 2027

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships: European Fellowships

European Commission · for fellows hosted at a European university

Your European host leads, and you may spend up to half of the fellowship on a secondment with Wits AHEAD.

Same 2027 call: 7 April to 8 September 2027

How to start

Three steps, and the first takes ten minutes.

  1. Tell us about your researchUse the postdoctoral fellowship form on the Join page. Name the scheme you have in mind and its deadline, and attach your CV.
  2. The Group Director answersEvery enquiry is read by the Group Director, with a reply within ten working days.
  3. We shape the application togetherIf the fit is right, we agree the question, the plan and the host's part of the application. Start early: several schemes need the host's commitment well before the deadline.

Bring your question to Johannesburg.

Whether your funding is secured, applied for, or still an idea, the first step is the same.
