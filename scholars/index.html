Why it matters

Knowledge is made everywhere. It should travel both ways.

The questions at the heart of Wits AHEAD, how artificial intelligence, analytics and data can serve human capability in higher education, are being worked on in many places at once. Too often the answers travel one way, and students and early-career researchers in Africa meet the leading work only on the page. AHEAD Global Scholars is how the group changes that, on purpose, one visit at a time.

01

Our questions are global

Teacher education, student success and the responsible use of AI are being rethought in Kingston, Helsinki, Lagos and Johannesburg at the same time. Our candidates should meet that work in conversation, and test their own ideas against it.

02

Africa is where knowledge is made, not only received

Visiting scholars come to learn as much as to share. The conversation runs both ways, and African classrooms, universities and policies shape the questions we ask together.

03

Doctorates grow through people

A study is shaped by the scholars a candidate meets. One good question, one honest reading of a proposal, one introduction can change its course. Every visit gives our doctoral and master's candidates time with the scholar on their own work.

04

A visit should leave something behind

Too many guest lectures end when the meeting closes. Every AHEAD Global Scholar leaves one agreed piece of work, such as a paper, a proposal or a joint project, and it is recorded here so the partnership can be seen and continued.

The point is not a lecture. It is a relationship that changes what our candidates can ask, and what we can build together.The purpose of AHEAD Global Scholars, Wits AHEAD Research Group
The programme

What every visit brings

AHEAD Global Scholars is a standing programme of the Wits AHEAD Research Group, Wits School of Education. It brings leading scholars into the life of the group, so that candidates, researchers and partners learn with them, and the work continues after the visit.

A public Global Conversation

Every scholar opens with one AHEAD Global Conversation: online, free and open to everyone who registers, so teachers and students across the continent can take part, not only those in the room.

Time with candidates

Conversations with Wits AHEAD doctoral and master's candidates about their questions, methods and writing, in person while the scholar is with us.

One agreed collaboration

Each visit ends with one shared piece of work that outlasts it: a paper, a proposal or a joint project.

For Wits AHEAD candidates and members

How to make the most of a visit

  1. Before

    Register, read one piece of the scholar's work, and bring one question from your own research.

  2. During

    Join on time, with your camera on where you can. Ask in the chat or aloud: this is a conversation.

  3. After

    Book a one-to-one while the scholar is with us. Within a week, send your supervisor a 300-word note on how the conversation shapes your study.

  4. Build

    Propose a joint output with the scholar, such as a paper, a seminar or a proposal, through the Director.

  5. Nominate

    Suggest the next AHEAD Global Scholar on the nomination form.
