The inaugural presenter

Prof Peter Chin

A science teacher turned teacher educator, Peter Chin has spent more than three decades at Queen's University asking how new teachers learn to think, and how teacher education can reach the communities that need it most.

As Associate Dean of Teacher Education from 2010 to 2026, he led multi-session Bachelor of Education programmes that widened access to the profession, built in partnership with Indigenous communities across Ontario and in French-language and technological education. His research, much of it funded by the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada, follows learning across the practicum, the workplace and the classroom. Since 2018 he has worked with 1 Million Teachers, Queen's partner in preparing teachers across Africa.

1 of 8Queen's, 2026

The 2026 Distinguished Service Award. One of eight people honoured across Queen's University this year, chosen by the University Council Executive Committee, for leadership that widened access to teacher education. Queen's celebrates him in Kingston on Friday 13 November.

About the programme

AHEAD Global Scholars

A standing programme of the Wits AHEAD Research Group that brings leading scholars from around the world into the life of the group, so that candidates, researchers and partners in Johannesburg learn with them, and the work continues after the visit.

A public Global Conversation

Every scholar opens with one AHEAD Global Conversation, online and open to all who register.

Time with candidates

Conversations with Wits AHEAD doctoral and master's candidates about their questions, methods and writing.

One agreed collaboration

Each visit ends with one shared piece of work that outlasts it: a paper, a proposal or a joint project.
